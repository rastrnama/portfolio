Core capabilities

More than a buy or sell alert.

01

Live Market Monitoring

Tracks selected markets and refreshes analysis as new confirmed market data becomes available.

02

Multi-Agent Analysis

Specialists independently study structure, setups, Wyckoff, technicals, liquidity, macro, news and statistics.

03

Smart Strategy Selection

Matches strategies to the observed market instead of forcing the same setup everywhere.

04

Chart Image Analysis

Supported plans can submit chart images for an additional visual market assessment.

05

Knowledge Feeding

Books, writeups and reports expand the agent's reference knowledge and can support refinement of its reasoning.

06

Historical Pre-Analysis

Replays context around a chosen historical date and maps plausible scenarios using information available before that point.

07

Risk Validation

Checks proposal logic and calculates sizing when the necessary account inputs are supplied.

08

Conflict Detection

Surfaces disagreement between technical, structural, macro, news, liquidity and statistical evidence.

09

No-Trade Detection

Rejects weak or unclear conditions instead of forcing a signal.
