Market Selection & Monitoring

Users choose the market before analysis begins. Initial markets are XAUUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD and USDJPY, with configurable watchlists for additional supported instruments. TradingView and Yahoo Finance adapters are supported, with explicit provider and instrument identification. Completed candles are used as confirmed evidence, while stale, malformed, missing and duplicate data are detected with controlled retries and configured fallbacks.

Specialized Analysis Team

Market Structure studies trends, ranges, swing points and structural breaks. Setup Detector / Price Action looks for second entries, continuations, failed entries, candlestick evidence, sweeps and retests. Wyckoff evaluates accumulation, distribution, springs, upthrusts and strength/weakness. Technicals studies momentum, volatility, compression, volume and indicators. Liquidity / SMC maps liquidity pools, sweeps, imbalances and fair-value gaps.

Macro adds broader conditions and risk flags. News Sentiment evaluates relevant headlines and event risk. Statistics checks historical evidence with sample-size warnings. The Planner matches evidence to strategies, the Risk Validator checks proposals and sizing, and the Manager turns the team's work into a readable final assessment.

Strategy Selection

The catalog includes second-entry continuation, Wyckoff phase setups, technical momentum, failed-break/reversal setups, range-edge fades, moving-average-aligned continuation, support/resistance and channel reactions, mean reversion with participation filters, and volatility-adjusted momentum. Each selection explains why it fits, what supports it, what contradicts it and what invalidates it.

Explained Trade Proposals

A proposal can include instrument, timeframe, direction, strategy, trend context, entry or trigger, stop loss, targets, reward-to-risk, confidence ranking, warnings, risk amount, position size when inputs permit, supporting/conflicting evidence, missing information and expiration. Confidence ranks evidence strength and is not a guaranteed win probability.

No-Trade Decisions

The system can stay out when no setup is valid, conditions are choppy, evidence conflicts, confirmation is insufficient, reward-to-risk is poor, data is unreliable, statistical support is weak or event risk is elevated.

Knowledge & Learning Workflow

Plans can ingest trading books plus user-provided or PEAK-provided expert writeups and reports. These materials expand the agent's reference knowledge. Scheduled review cycles can revisit stored analyses and mistakes to refine future reasoning. More reference material may improve context and coverage, but does not guarantee a higher win rate.

Autonomous AI

Autonomous mode is a deeper scheduled research workflow that can gather supported market and news data, coordinate specialists, review historical context, test scenarios, validate risk assumptions and produce a complete research report with minimal prompting. It can be customized by market, strategy, providers and delivery channel. The core PEAK TRADE assistant remains decision support rather than automatic trade execution.
